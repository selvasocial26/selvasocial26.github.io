Selva Sync

Selva Sync is a Windows desktop application that backs up selected folders from your computer to your own Google Drive.

How it works

  • You sign in with your Google account and give Selva Sync permission to manage only the files it creates in your Google Drive.
  • You choose which local folders to back up. Selva Sync uploads and updates copies of those files in your Drive.
  • Selva Sync cannot see or change any other files in your Google Drive.

Use of Google user data

Selva Sync requests the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which only allows access to files the app itself creates or that you open with it. This data is used solely to back up your files to your Drive. See our Privacy Policy for details.
