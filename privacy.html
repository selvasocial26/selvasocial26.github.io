Selva Sync – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how Selva Sync ("the app"), a Windows desktop application published by V. Selvakumar, handles your information.

Information the app accesses

  • Google account sign-in: The app uses Google OAuth to obtain an access token for your Google account. Your Google password is never seen or stored by the app.
  • Google Drive files: The app requests only the drive.file scope. This lets it create, read, update and delete only the files and folders that Selva Sync itself creates in your Google Drive. It cannot access any other files in your Drive.
  • Local files: The app reads the files in the local folders you choose, in order to upload them to your Drive.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide the backup feature: uploading your selected files to your own Google Drive and keeping those copies up to date.

Storage and sharing

  • OAuth tokens and app settings are stored locally on your computer only.
  • The app has no server of its own. Your files travel directly from your computer to Google Drive.
  • The publisher does not collect, store, sell or share your personal data or file contents with anyone.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking is used.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Selva Sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored tokens and settings. Backed-up files remain in your Google Drive under your control and can be deleted by you at any time.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.
